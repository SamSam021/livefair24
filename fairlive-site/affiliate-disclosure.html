Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: August 19, 2026

LiveFair24 is free for you to use. We keep it running through affiliate commissions, which we want to be upfront about.

How it works

When you click through from LiveFair24 to buy a ticket or book a hotel, and you complete that purchase with the provider, we may earn a commission from that provider. This is standard practice for sites like ours and is how we're able to offer ticket and hotel search for free.

This never changes your price

Any commission we earn comes from the ticket seller or hotel provider, not from you. The price you're shown on LiveFair24 is the same price you'd get going directly to the provider — clicking through to buy doesn't cost you anything extra, and it doesn't change which listing we show you first. Prices are pulled live from our ticketing partner and update continuously.

What this means for what you see

When you click a "Get tickets" or "Book hotel" link on LiveFair24, you'll be taken to the provider's own website to complete the purchase. Your booking contract is with that provider, not with LiveFair24 — they handle your payment, your booking confirmation, and any customer service related to that purchase.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you want to know more about how a specific price or link works, get in touch. For how we handle your data specifically (including any tracking related to affiliate links), see our Privacy Policy.
