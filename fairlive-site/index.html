How LiveFair24 works

Three steps to the cheapest concert trip.

1. Search any concert

Type an artist name, city, or event. We find every upcoming concert and match it across all providers.

2. Compare ticket prices

We show you total price including all fees from every seller — not misleading headline prices. The cheapest option is always at the top.

3. Add hotel + total cost

We find hotels near the venue, calculate distance, and show you the complete trip cost: ticket + hotel + local transport.
