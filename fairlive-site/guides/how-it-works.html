How LiveFair24 works

LiveFair24 shows concert ticket prices and pairs them with nearby hotel prices, so you can see the real total cost of going to a show — not just the face value of a ticket — before you commit to anything.

1. We look up ticket prices

For every event, we fetch live ticket prices and refresh them continuously.

2. We match nearby hotels

We pull hotel availability and pricing around each venue, sorted by distance, so you can weigh a closer hotel against a cheaper one further out.

3. We show total trip cost

Ticket price plus nearby hotel price plus a local transport estimate — one number, so you can weigh cities and dates against each other.
