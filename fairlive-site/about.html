About LiveFair24

LiveFair24 is a concert and sports ticket search site. We show real, live ticket prices for events, pair them with nearby hotel options, and add the two together so you can see the actual total cost of going to a show — before you commit to anything.

What we do

Ticket prices update continuously from our ticketing partner, and we pull hotel availability and pricing for the area around each venue. Instead of comparing a ticket price on one site and a hotel price on another, LiveFair24 puts both together so the real cost of a trip — ticket plus a place to stay — is visible in one place.

You can read more about exactly how this works on our How it works page.

How we make money

LiveFair24 is free to use. When you buy a ticket or book a hotel through a link on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission from that booking — at no extra cost to you. This never affects the prices you're shown; every price comes directly from the ticket or hotel provider. Full details are on our affiliate disclosure page.
