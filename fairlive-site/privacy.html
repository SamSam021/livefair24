Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What this page covers

This page explains what cookies LiveFair24 uses, why, and how you can control them. LiveFair24 is a ticket and hotel search service — we don't sell tickets or hotel rooms directly; when you click through to a seller, that seller's own privacy policy applies to your purchase.

Cookies we use

Essential

Required for the site to function — for example, keeping an admin login session active. These can't be disabled and don't track you across other sites.

Preference

Remembers choices you've made on this site, like your cookie consent decision itself, so we don't ask again on every visit.

Third-party content

Our venue maps are provided by OpenStreetMap via Leaflet, and fonts are served by Google Fonts. Loading these pages may involve requests to those third parties, subject to their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies from the banner shown on your first visit. You can also clear cookies at any time through your browser's settings, which will reset your choice and show the banner again on your next visit.

Data we collect

If you sign up for a price-drop alert, we store the email address you provide and the event you're watching, solely to send you that alert. We don't sell or share this data with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every alert email.

Questions

Contact us via the details on our About page with any privacy questions.
